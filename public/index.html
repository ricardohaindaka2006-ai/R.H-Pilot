HOW IT PROTECTS YOU

Risk & profit rules

01Planned risk is budgeted

The engine constrains planned stop risk before entry. Gaps and slippage can exceed that budget; losses are recorded in full.

02Stops can tighten, not insure a profit

Break-even and trailing triggers tighten the stop. A price gap can still turn an unrealised gain into a loss.

03Nothing runs unsupervised

A daily loss limit and an emergency close are always one click away on the trading desk.

SPRINGBOK TRADING ENGINE

Welcome to R.H-Pilot

A short guide before you start. You will only see this once.

  1. Tell the bot your balance. Enter what you actually have — even $2. Every limit scales to it and grows as your account grows.
  2. Pick your pair, or accept the suggestion. The bot highlights the pair with the strongest signals right now.
  3. Choose a risk profile. Conservative is the default paper budget, not a proven profitable setting. Every field stays editable underneath.
  4. Press Start engine once. The server then manages paper entries and exits automatically while it is running; you do not need to approve each trade or keep this page open. Deriv Options and MT5/CFD real orders remain disabled.
  5. Watch the wallet. Paper P/L is separate from the linked broker balance. No broker contract is bought by the engine.
Your safety net
